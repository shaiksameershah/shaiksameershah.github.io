The person behind the pages

Curious by nature.
Clear by design.

I work at the intersection of data, AI, governance, and product strategy, with a particular interest in how organisations turn complex technology capabilities into reliable, business-aligned products.

Over the past 10 years, my work has spanned financial services, technology, consulting, and e-commerce. I began my career working with predictive models, forecasting, anomaly detection, and risk analytics. Over time, my focus shifted from building analytical solutions to understanding the broader systems that make them useful at scale: the data they depend on, the governance that makes them trustworthy, and the operating models that connect technology with business outcomes.

Today, I work on Data Quality Issue Management at JPMorgan Chase, supporting enterprise data governance and regulatory expectations aligned with BCBS 239 and Basel. My broader experience includes AI product management, AI governance, workforce transformation, risk analytics, forecasting, automation, and cloud data modernisation across organisations including ServiceNow, Wells Fargo, Accenture, and Amazon.

What interests me most is not AI in isolation, but the organisational capability required to make it work. I am interested in how data products, governance frameworks, semantic layers, and well-designed operating models can work together to make enterprise AI more scalable, accountable, and useful. For me, the challenge is as much about ownership, decision-making, and organisational design as it is about technology.

I hold a certificate in Business Strategy from ISB and am pursuing an MSc in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University. Alongside my professional work, I write and explore ideas around Enterprise AI, data governance, data quality, and the future of enterprise operating models. I see research and writing as ways to examine practical problems more deeply, challenge established assumptions, and develop ideas that extend beyond the immediate needs of a project.

Over the long term, I want to contribute to how organisations design and govern AI-enabled enterprises through product leadership, applied research, and thought leadership.
