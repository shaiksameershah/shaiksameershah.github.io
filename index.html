The person behind the pages

Curious by nature.
Clear by design.

I work at the intersection of data, AI, governance, and product strategy, with a particular interest in how organizations turn complex technology capabilities into reliable, business-aligned products.

Over the past 10 years, my work has spanned financial services, technology, consulting, and e-commerce. I began my career working with predictive models, forecasting, anomaly detection, and risk analytics. Over time, my focus shifted from building analytical solutions to understanding the broader systems that make them useful at scale: the data they depend on, the governance that makes them trustworthy, and the operating models that connect technology with business outcomes.
